# ISO 27001 Certification Roadmap (18 Months)  
### Veridian Resources (Fictional Case Study for Learning & Portfolio Demonstration Purposes Only)

This roadmap outlines a structured, risk‑based approach to achieving ISO 27001 certification.  
The sequencing reflects expert‑level consideration of mining‑sector operational constraints and regulatory obligations.

---

# 📅 Phase 1 — High‑Risk Remediation (0–3 Months)

| Objective | Key Activities | Outcomes |
|-----------|----------------|----------|
| Access Control Remediation | Remove dormant accounts; enforce MFA; implement provisioning workflow | Reduced unauthorised access risk |
| Incident Response Establishment | Develop IR plan; define roles; implement reporting channels | Ability to respond to OT/IT incidents |
| Critical Asset Identification | Build initial asset register | Visibility of critical systems |

---

# 📅 Phase 2 — Governance & Policy Foundation (3–6 Months)

| Objective | Key Activities | Outcomes |
|-----------|----------------|----------|
| ISMS Governance | Define roles, responsibilities, reporting cadence | Executive oversight established |
| Policy Development | Create and approve ISO‑aligned policies | Formal governance baseline |
| Risk Register | Establish enterprise risk register | Centralised risk visibility |

---

# 📅 Phase 3 — Control Implementation & Embedding (6–12 Months)

| Objective | Key Activities | Outcomes |
|-----------|----------------|----------|
| Asset Management Maturity | Classification, ownership, lifecycle controls | Full asset governance |
| Supplier Security | Supplier policy, contract clauses, onboarding controls | Reduced third‑party risk |
| Logging & Monitoring | Centralised logging, alerting, review processes | Improved detection capability |

---

# 📅 Phase 4 — Audit Readiness & Certification (12–18 Months)

| Objective | Key Activities | Outcomes |
|-----------|----------------|----------|
| Internal Audit | Conduct ISO‑aligned internal audit | Identify gaps before certification |
| Management Review | Executive review of ISMS performance | Governance validation |
| External Audit Preparation | Evidence collection, auditor engagement | Certification readiness |
